After the winter solstice

Torrejón de Ardoz, 23 December 2025

After the winter solstice, light that seemed gone begins its quiet return.

It never truly leaves. It only dims, rests, and gathers itself.

Each day it rises again, slow and patient, reclaiming the sky and its place among us.
